The thing that finally made sense to me this week is why bill.py has no input() or
print() in it. On my first submission I had the math right in the module but I
called split_evenly(subtotal, people) in console.py instead of split_evenly(total,
people), so the per-person amount ignored the tip. The unit tests were all green
and I still had a bug — that's the difference between a unit test (checks one
function in isolation) and an integration test (checks that the console interface
actually uses the module correctly). Reading the failing assertion ("$15.00" not in
output) is what pointed me at the right line.

I struggled most with the Streamlit dashboard. I left it half-done the first time
because I didn't understand how the AppTest finds widgets — it turns out the key=
argument on number_input and slider is what the test uses to look them up, and the
st.metric tiles are what it reads the results from. Once I mirrored the console
logic (same four bill functions) it passed.
